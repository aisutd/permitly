286.11-017 Application fee and license fee.
Each application shall be accompanied by a nonrefundable application fee in the amount
of five hundred dollars ($500) and a nonrefundable license fee of five hundred dollars
($500) if application is made on or before March 31, or the next business day if March 31
falls on a weekend or holiday. If application is made after March 31, the license fee shall
be two hundred fifty dollars ($250). The license shall remain in force through September
30 of the same year the license is issued.
